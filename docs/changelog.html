v0.0.4 October 7, 2026

Shared

  • Cut, Flash, Rock Smash, Strength, Surf, Fly, Dive, and Waterfall are now unlocked for field use by Emerald's original badge progression instead of learned HM moves.
  • Added a compact FIELD submenu for manual access to unlocked utilities, including Flash and Fly.
  • Preserved terrain, follower, map, link, and Regi-puzzle restrictions.
  • Removed Pokémon-specific HM-use messages and suppressed arbitrary Pokémon portrait banners for these utilities.
  • Dig, Teleport, Sweet Scent, and other non-HM field moves remain tied to learned moves.
