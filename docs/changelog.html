EMERALD: LEGENDS // CHANGELOG
v0.0.23

Build history

What changed?

Release and Debug share the same Legends gameplay and content unless noted otherwise. Use the tabs to see the history for the build you actually play or test.

v0.0.23 October 8, 2026

Shared

  • A home wardrobe with outfit, scarf and navy jacket previews; Apply confirms and B cancels.
  • Five additional scarf colors and an optional jacket, matching both genders’ overworld/battle art and local Trainer Cards.
  • Buy a placeable wardrobe ornament for ₽3,000 at Pretty Petal; use it in your own secret base.
  • Existing saves keep their skin tones. Native diving suits, NPCs, remote art and decoration/save layouts are preserved.
No variant-specific differences; developer tools remain disabled.

v0.0.22 October 8, 2026

Shared

  • Regional dynamic rain, clouds and occasional storms across ordinary Hoenn towns/routes; wetter rainforest routes.
  • Rainforest/upland fog, with morning/night and autumn boosts; winter flurries on Routes 114–116.
  • Saved 20-minute playtime forecast periods and smooth native transitions. Desert, volcanic, indoor and story weather are preserved.
  • Clear nights show a moon and twinkling star in the pause panel. Existing saves are supported.
No variant-specific differences; developer tools remain disabled.

v0.0.21 October 8, 2026

Shared

  • Da Bug’s follower is now half-size, centered and grounded in its native frames.
  • Smaller PC/party icons match native small Pokémon; its large PC/Pokédex preview is slightly reduced.
  • Its battle back view sits 8 pixels lower. Normal/shiny colors and red eye glimmers are preserved.
  • The one-time PC gift, moves, cry and 1-in-10 shiny roll are unchanged; existing saves keep their Da Bug.
No variant-specific differences; developer tools remain disabled.

v0.0.20 October 8, 2026

Shared

  • Meet Da Bug: a single-stage Bug/Grass leafhopper with green leaf wings, red eyes and an autumn amber shiny with teal eyes.
  • Your PC receives one level-5 gift with Tackle, Leer, Mean Look and Absorb. Existing saves receive it on continue in their first free box slot; full storage retries later.
  • Only this gift has a 1-in-10 shiny roll. It cannot respawn after withdrawal or release; no wild encounters are added.
  • Includes native battle/PC/follower art, a cute chirp, swaying red-eye glimmer, the approved Pokédex entry and a Bug/Grass level-up progression.
Same Da Bug gameplay in both builds; developer tools remain disabled in Release. Both BPS patches passed byte-for-byte verification.

v0.0.19 October 8, 2026

Shared

  • Added Lavender for both genders, with plum shading, cream scarf details and matching overworld and battle art.
  • Adventure Options offers Gen 1–9 starters, Special (Happiny/Wattrel/Mankey), and Random (three distinct unevolved Pokémon that can evolve).
  • Rivals use the matching generation counter; Special/Random use Zorua, evolving to Zoroark at level 30.
  • Rescue lineups stay fixed; later edits apply to a future New Game. Existing saves and default Gen 3 teams retain their original behavior.
No variant-specific differences; developer tools remain disabled.

v0.0.18 October 8, 2026

Shared

  • NEXT PAGE replaces CANCEL on both Options pages. Select it and press A to cycle pages; L/R shortcuts remain available.
  • A permanent A: NEXT / B: SAVE hint stays visible regardless of version length. B saves all settings and exits from any row.
  • Preserved the original window dimensions, pending settings and L=A navigation behavior.
No variant-specific differences; developer tools remain disabled.

v0.0.17 October 8, 2026

Shared

  • Stunky now chases the professor in the opening Route 101 rescue and appears in the level-2 battle, using native Stunky walking sprites and colors.
  • Starter choices, rescue movement and story progression are unchanged. Existing saves remain compatible.
No variant-specific differences; developer tools remain disabled.

v0.0.16 October 8, 2026

Shared polish

  • Naming icons reflect your chosen skin and outfit; intro prompts clear the entire text box and animated dialogue restores its own glyph colors.
  • Added Yellow for both genders, removed gloves from custom outfits, refined battle poses and corrected May’s face detail. Emerald retains its gloves.
  • Grass animations match the season. Standard winter grass tufts clear underfoot until a full map reload, preserving encounters and terrain behavior.
No variant-specific gameplay differences; developer tools remain disabled.

v0.0.15 October 8, 2026

Shared

  • Choose five skin tones and three outfits per gender after choosing gender in Birch’s introduction: Emerald, Trail and Sport.
  • A live sprite preview appears at the bottom right above the dialogue. B returns to the previous choice.
  • Your saved appearance carries into overworld movement, local battle poses and your Trainer Card. Existing saves retain the original Emerald look; link opponents retain native sprites.
