PAUSE MENU · SECOND PAGE

Your quests and achievements

Open the pause menu and press Right or Left. The small arrow points toward its other page. Choose QUEST LOG or ACHIEVEMENTS; your Adventure Log is still available.

33 quests with automatic checks

Follow Hoenn's main adventure, all nine current Kanto survey chapters and the Hoenn Regigigas investigation. Seven basic side quests cover existing optional goals. Main Story, Side Quests and Completed views keep the entries easy to browse. Checks update from your actual game progress, including progress recovered from earlier Legends saves.

95 achievements, with berry rewards

Earn achievements through badges, expeditions, catching, exploring, battles, eggs, evolution, berries, fishing, contests, DexNav and the Battle Tower. Each detail page shows its requirement and reward. A small upper-left notice names each new unlock and disappears without interrupting movement; notices wait while menus, scripts or location banners are active.

Press A on an unlocked achievement to send its berries to PC ITEM STORAGE. A temporary popup confirms the berries were sent. Unlocking an achievement never claims its reward automatically. Each reward can be claimed once. If the PC is full, make room and retry; the claim remains available. Save your game to keep the berries and claimed status.

More room for your collection

There are now 20 Pokémon boxes (600 slots) and 150 PC item slots. Bag pockets hold 150 Items, 60 Key Items, 32 Balls, 128 TMs/HMs and 80 Berries. The Battle Pyramid keeps its own challenge bag.

Existing Legends saves migrate automatically. Once you save with this version, continue using v0.0.43 or newer. The expanded inventory uses lossless compression within the normal save size. If an unusually detailed collection cannot fit the save budget, saving stops before changing either previous save; the technical notes describe this limit.
