The Champion's first visit

Across the sea to Vermilion

Become Hoenn Champion, then speak to the gentleman at Lilycove Harbor. Kanto's Pokémon League has sent an invitation. Your Invite Ticket lets the nearby sailor take you to Vermilion Port and back, whenever you wish. Keep it: each crossing requires the ticket, and it is never consumed.

Two Fan Club members welcome you on your first arrival. Vermilion has three homes, a Fan Club, a working Pokémon Center and a Mart stocked for postgame travel. Cooltrainer ALEX waits on the city waterfront with six Kanto Pokémon at Lv. 60–64. His challenge is optional; you can decline, heal and return later.

To go home, return to the port and speak to the sailor at the end of the gangway. The ship remains available. This chapter opens Vermilion; storm repairs close Routes 6 and 11, and Lt. Surge is away helping the crews. Onward travel and the Gym are planned for later chapters.

Test with the Debug menu

Use the Debug build. In the overworld, hold R and press START. Numeric selectors use Left/Right to choose a digit, Up/Down to change it, A to confirm and B to go back. All IDs here are decimal. Use a separate testing save; Game clear alone does not complete all Emerald story events.

  1. On an existing Champion save, leave Game clear enabled. On a new test save, choose Flags & Vars… → Toggle Game clear to enable it. Debug's title-screen SELECT starts native Quickstart. Give X… → Pokémon (Basic) can prepare a team around Lv. 60–65.
  2. Choose Utilities… → Warp to map warp… and enter 13 → 10 → 0. Walk up into Lilycove Harbor and talk to the gentleman, then the sailor near the entrance. This tests the real ticket and travel progression.
  3. If a fresh Debug setup has the native ship hidden, use Flags & Vars… → Set Flag XYZ… → 861. If it shows TRUE, press A once to make it FALSE, then leave and re-enter the harbor. This is Emerald's native Lilycove ship-visibility flag.
  4. Decline a sailing, then accept. Confirm the first welcome plays and the ticket remains in Key Items. Walk north into Vermilion; visit its Center, Mart, homes and Fan Club.
  5. Talk to ALEX on the city waterfront. Decline first, then prepare/heal and accept. Inspect victory dialogue and later conversations. On a separate attempt, lose and confirm recovery at Vermilion's Center.
  6. Go south to the port and talk to the sailor at the end of the gangway. Decline, then accept passage home. Sail from Lilycove again: the welcome should not repeat. Save/reload in both regions and repeat the journey.

Repeat specific tests

Give a ticket: Give X… → Give item XYZ… → Item 874 → Quantity 1. Use the normal gentleman's invitation when checking progression.

Replay the welcome: Flags & Vars… → Set Flag XYZ… → 621 (0x26D). If TRUE, press A once to make it FALSE. Leave and re-enter Vermilion Port.

Reset ALEX's victory: use the same menu with 2135 (0x857). Make it FALSE and speak to him again. Other trainer victories remain intact.

The flag selector toggles on A; if the target flag already reads FALSE, leave it alone.
