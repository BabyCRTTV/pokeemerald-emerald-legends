Understanding encounter rates

Spawn % is a Pokémon's share of encounters for that particular method after an encounter happens. It is not a chance per step.

Land, Surf, Rock Smash, Old Rod, Good Rod and Super Rod have separate weighted tables. Where the same species appears in several slots, the percentage is combined and the minimum/maximum levels are displayed.

These are static tables. Story conditions, temporary overrides and other special encounters may behave differently. The original FireRed encounter tables for unopened locations are excluded.
