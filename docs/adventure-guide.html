ADVENTURE LOG

A little notebook for your journey

Choose ADVENTURE LOG in the pause menu. Its cream pages keep your recent story milestones, locations, played dates, accumulated play time and one recent catch. Key Hoenn and Wing Survey events are noted automatically.

The book retains the 32 newest notes. Repeated captures and saves refresh a field note between milestones rather than filling it with duplicates. Save normally to keep your notes between sessions.

How DAY counts your adventure

The new DAY row sits above the clock, weather and season. It counts dates you actually play, not dates you spend away from the game. Four hours today and three hours on a later date mean Day 2 and seven total play hours. Several sessions on the same date stay on the same day; returning after a week adds just one played day.

Existing saves begin their notebook at Day 1 because earlier played dates cannot be recovered reliably. Your existing Pokemon, badges and story progress stay intact. A missing RTC pauses the day counter; play time still accumulates. Clock rollbacks do not create duplicate days.
