# When Morning Never Comes

Introduced in v0.0.77. A worried father’s call after Regidrago leads to a rescue beyond waking Hoenn.

## The call and investigation

After Regidrago is caught or defeated, eight free outdoor steps trigger a one-time unknown-number call. Elias received your number from Briney and asks for help with his daughter Mira. Escape, loss or merely awakening Regidrago does not qualify. The call yields to existing higher-priority field events and cannot interrupt a cave, dream or forced movement.

Their new home is just east of Lilycove's Trainer Fan Club. Before the call Mira is awake; during the quest she is tucked into bed and her father explains what happened. Speak to the old caretaker on Mt. Pyre's first floor. She provides the Lunar Wing as a permanent Key Item and retains her original Cleanse Tag grant. A full Key Items pocket leaves the wing available for a later visit.

## Following the dream

Interact with Mira's bed while carrying the wing and accept the prompt. The empty version of the room leads into deserted Lilycove. The empty chair has different dialogue and a native sound; the other doors are unresponsive. Mira's echo near the coast points toward the neighboring house, whose door opens onto a moonless shore. The original room's empty bed always offers a safe way to wake up. Both visible home exits and both shore forest passages work.

The dream uses private palettes and existing Emerald assemblies. The shoreline's layout and every original tile pixel remain native; Darkrai's temporary silhouette footprint changes only runtime collision and clears when it is caught. Its field sprite, native legendary battle and cold water backdrop share the same setting. No random encounters interrupt the dream.

## Darkrai and the return

A level-80 Darkrai waits on the pier. Declining leaves the encounter available. Fleeing or losing leaves the quest unfinished and retryable, with the wing retained. Catching or defeating Darkrai wakes Mira and returns you to the real room. Her father then completes the quest. Defeat preserves a later capture opportunity: use the bed and wing to revisit the shore. After capture the field encounter is absent and its full footprint is walkable. Mira never has to fall asleep again for that revisit.
