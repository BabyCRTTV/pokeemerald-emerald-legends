# Wyrmtide Isle

Introduced in v0.0.76. Mr. Briney can take you to a small island west of Dewford, where an ancient seal hides the Maker's last unfinished creation.

## Starting the expedition

Calm Regigigas and finish the Weather Institute report. After eight ordinary outdoor steps, an unknown-number Pokénav call reveals Mr. Briney and his discovery. Visit his Dewford dock and choose Wyrmtide Isle. His original coastal routes remain available when the story permits them. Briney offers a return voyage from the island before and after solving its seal.

The three shore tablets point toward the continent's mover. Bring a real, non-egg Regigigas in your party; a fainted Regigigas also qualifies. Opening the seal is permanent, so storing Regigigas afterward cannot trap you outside.

## The descent

Four connected cave chambers lead to Regidrago's sanctuary. Complete native rock openings, fitted stair flights and shaded terraces mark the route; upper stairwells return to the previous room. The tidal gallery has a small animated pool. Each carved tablet is readable and has distinct lore. Flash is not required.

Ordinary cave encounters include Golbat, Graveler, Sableye, Mawile, Nosepass, Carbink and Onix. The encounter rate is deliberately restrained. Dragon percentages below are shares of wild encounters, not per-step probabilities.

| Chamber | Levels | Dragon at 4% | Dragon at 1% |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Descent 1 | 48–54 | Bagon | Gible |
| Descent 2 | 50–56 | Noibat | Axew |
| Descent 3 | 53–59 | Druddigon | Deino |
| Descent 4 | 55–61 | Gabite | Fraxure |

The sanctuary has no random encounters.

## Regidrago and the return voyage

A sleeping level-75 Regidrago waits beneath the final inscription. Speak to it and choose whether to awaken it. Escaping or losing leaves it available. Defeating it calms it and permits another challenge. Capturing it removes the field encounter permanently. Report to Briney after defeat or capture to complete **The Maker's Last Dream**; return travel remains available.

Existing saves keep their Pokémon, inventory, wardrobe, seasons and story state. Wyrmtide appends its maps and quest without renumbering published identifiers and uses audited unused flags. Older Dewford NPC caches receive only the two expedition actors; existing actors and movements are preserved. Use the normal in-game battery save when updating.
