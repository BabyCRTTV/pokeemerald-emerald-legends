# Prepared battle appearance — v0.0.44

Battle backs use fixed clothing. Overworld/front portraits still use the full wardrobe. Selected skin tone remains active in battle. The ten supplied female sheets map directly to saved hairstyle IDs 1–10, matching Pixie through Twin Braids. Original (0) and male battle backs use native Emerald graphics. Male prepared sheets are not included in this revision.

## Authoring and palettes

`tools/legends/battle_sheets.json` is the name/ID/source manifest. Original uploads live under `graphics/legends/battle_sources`. The presentation sheets supply hair shapes and contours only. `import_battle_sheets.py` removes their background/grid, extracts the Natural-row hair regions and fits the original logical pixel grid to native May's eye landmarks. The original Emerald body, face, eyes and four pose geometries supply the trainer. Eye-based alignment prevents an arm touching the cheek from shifting a hairstyle when a skin bounding box grows. Pose-specific glove/cuff regions and actual face contours replace broad head/hand rectangles; collar colors and body outlines are retained, and isolated native nape/forelock remnants are cleared. The release arm segment formerly hidden by May’s swept hair is restored with skin materials 1–3, behind the face and in front of the hair. The whole supplied character is never scaled to fit the arm span. The earlier undersized whole-character conversion is superseded.

The generated indexed source sheets and 81920-byte frame bank are in `graphics/legends/battle_may`. Skin slots 1–3 reuse the existing five skin-tone ramps. Hair has a clean three-shade ramp in slots 4, 7 and 11, with native black outlines. Body-only warm edge pixels are consolidated into fixed shade 8, and belt greens into fixed slot 10; hair color therefore cannot tint skin edges, gloves, clothing or the belt. Eight hair ramps retain Natural, Black, Dark Brown, Chestnut, Auburn, Honey, Blond and Silver. All art is static native tiled 4bpp; there is no runtime head/body layering.

Native Hoenn animation uses May's wind-up, cast, release and idle at indices 0,1,2,3. Corresponding supplied hair poses are source frames 2,3,4,1. Emerald continues to animate the moving Ball separately; the detached illustration Ball in the supplied sheet is not imported.

## Runtime connection

`legends_battle_appearance.c` supplies current back images/palettes through the native `GetTrainerBackPicImage` and `GetTrainerBackPicPalette` helpers. Both the sprite constructor and buffer-copy path use the same fixed art. Battle palettes are rebuilt from current staged/saved appearance settings on each request, so wardrobe Apply, Cancel and later save reloads use the authoritative current choices. There is no cached stale hairstyle.

Battle choices ignore outfit/accessory/costume IDs when choosing the fixed uniform. They retain the correct trainer gender. Non-Legends trainer IDs fall back to their native art/palette. Front portraits and field compositors keep their existing behavior, including the approved May shoulder cleanup.

## Validation

The prepared-sprite regression regenerates the checked-in art and runs the actual C image/palette selector across all ten cuts, eight hair colors and five skin tones. It checks stable hairstyle IDs, frame sizes, preserved native face skin/eyes and lower-body cells, complete raised cuffs, one connected silhouette in every frame, native routing, fixed uniforms for every wardrobe trainer ID, independent non-skin colors and a simulated wardrobe cut/color/tone change. Existing footwear, appearance, intro-hair, wardrobe and sprite-mask regressions also apply. Local Debug compiled with GCC 13.2.1. mGBA 0.10.2 rendered all ten imported cuts and all five skin tones from the compiled image/palette selectors. Actual wardrobe controls changed Short Bob/Black to Twin Braids/Blond, left saved choices unchanged while staging, and saved the new choices only on APPLY. The next normal wild battle displayed Twin Braids/Blond/Tan in the fixed red uniform; the native moving Ball and trainer throw completed and Pikachu entered normally.
