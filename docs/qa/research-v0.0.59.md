## Scope and safeguards

Thirty research requests (18 Birch, 12 Devon) append stable IDs 6–29 and quest IDs 39–62. The original 39 quest entries retain their exact hash and all 95 achievements retain their IDs/layout. Both capstones require Champion status and their patron's earlier reports; Devon's twelfth grants exactly one Master Ball. Native gifts, deliveries, Pokedex rating and story movement remain intact. Six rows plus Back preserve the established native menu footprint, with group selection for larger lists. Only one research request can be active globally; only its owner can report/abandon it.

The v1 B7 reader ignores new words and does not mutate saves. Successful state writes upgrade to B8, retaining selection/completions/notes and zeroing one high completion word at 0x407A and converting the old active-note bit into a boolean. Invalid older state initializes only on acceptance; full-PC submission changes nothing. No save structure, sector, packed-storage header, journal layout, artwork, map geometry, wardrobe or seasonal rule changed.

The first proposed high word at 0x406E conflicted with a FireRed Safari alias cleared on Continue. Unmodified Release caught the conflict before any commit/publication. A symbolic-alias review also protected the existing Seafoam entry word. The final format uses only 0x407A for high completion bits, plus an observation boolean in the existing note word. All Hoenn/FireRed symbolic aliases were resolved and scanned; the host regression rejects future uses of those aliases. Native Continue then verified high-word persistence.
